# Code of Conduct

Glass Box adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. Its full text applies to every space of the project:
issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews.

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good intent, give and accept
constructive criticism gracefully, and focus on what is best for the
community. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing
other people's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer
privately through the contact options on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/xmavgithub). Reports are
handled confidentially. Do not use public issues to report conduct problems.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not follow
this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is
harmful, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
